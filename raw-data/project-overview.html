Why a proxy?

Apps often show different content, prices and permissions in different countries. A proxy puts your session in the same region as the store listing, so what you review is what local users get. It also stops one person from reviewing the same app from many accounts.

Proxy fees pay for the connection only and are not part of your reward. Every review you finish pays ₦600 regardless of plan.
