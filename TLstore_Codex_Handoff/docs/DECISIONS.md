# TLstore — Open Decisions

Codex should update this file as decisions are confirmed. Do not silently choose financial rules.

1. Brand and domains: confirm final brand spelling and production domains.
2. Launch platforms: Android only at first, or Android and iOS together?
3. User verification: email, phone, identity verification, or a combination?
4. App assessment reward trigger: manual moderation, sampling, or approved automated rules?
5. Advertiser model: direct advertiser contracts or integrations with a third-party ad provider?
6. Ad event eligibility: which provider-authorized events can be rewarded?
7. Membership: does a package only grant access, or also configure limits? Avoid guaranteed-return promises.
8. Package pricing/currency: confirm NGN/USD behavior, checkout currency and settlement currency.
9. Reward policy: pending period, rejection reasons, appeal period and reversal rules.
10. Withdrawal: minimum, fees, supported countries, payout methods, processing SLA and approval thresholds.
11. Referrals: confirm qualifying event and commission base for proposed 5% / 2% / 1% rates.
12. Rankings: qualification period, measured metrics, reward funding source and reversal behavior.
13. Regional testing: authorized regions and permitted test arrangements; no geographic circumvention.
14. Proxy/access plans: whether needed at launch and how authorized regional testing is provided.
15. Data retention: assessment evidence retention, account deletion, privacy policy and consent requirements.
16. Support: ticket-only, live chat, or both?
17. Admin governance: which roles can approve withdrawals and adjustments, and what limits apply?
18. Hosting: provider, region, backups, monitoring and deployment strategy.
19. Payment providers: confirm provider account onboarding and sandbox availability.
20. Legal/compliance review: confirm obligations for payments, memberships, rewards, referrals, privacy and consumer protection before production activation.
